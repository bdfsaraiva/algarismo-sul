Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The Portuguese version prevails.

1. Data controller

Algarismo Sul, with offices at Rua D. João de Castro, n.º 84C, 2800-104 Almada, Portugal, is the controller of the personal data collected through this website and in the course of its accounting, tax, payroll and advisory services. Contact:geral@algarismosul.pt.

2. Data we process

  • Identification and contact details you send us voluntarily by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Tax, accounting and employment data needed to perform our service agreement.
  • Technical request data (IP address, date and time, browser type) logged by our hosting provider for security and operation.

3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Performance of a contract: providing the services you engage us for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Legal obligations: filings with the Tax Authority, Social Security and other public bodies (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Legitimate interest: answering enquiries, managing client relationships and website security (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Retention

Tax and accounting records are kept for the periods required by Portuguese tax and commercial law (generally 10 years). Contact data with no contractual relationship is deleted after 3 years without interaction.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website uses no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are served by the site itself, with no third-party requests. We only store in your browser (localStorage) the fact that you dismissed the cookie notice.

The Google map only loads if you click “Show map”. From then on, Google may collect data and set cookies under its own privacy policy.

6. Processors and international transfers

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. This service, like Google (if you enable the map) and Meta (if you contact us on WhatsApp), may involve transfers outside the European Union under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. For our services we use accounting software and cloud storage bound by processing agreements under Art. 28 GDPR.

7. Your rights

You may at any time exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection by emailing us. You may also lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (cnpd.pt).

8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, such as access control, encryption in transit and regular backups, to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration or destruction.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect legal or operational changes. The version published on this page is always the one in force.

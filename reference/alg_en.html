07 · Frequently asked questions

What people ask
before signing on.

We have gathered the most common questions. If yours isn't here, call or send us a message — we reply the same day.

What accounting services do you offer in Almada? +
We provide general and analytical accounting, tax advisory, payroll processing, business set-up support and integrated management solutions. The goal is to optimise your business finances and ensure full compliance with tax obligations.
Is switching accountants a simple process? +
It is a relatively simple process, especially for you. The Order of Certified Accountants has a code of conduct that ensures the handover is handled directly between accountants, with no noise on your side.
Do I have to come in to deliver invoices and paperwork? +
No. You can scan documents and drop them into our shared cloud folder, or — if you prefer — we can arrange a monthly pickup at no extra cost. The process can be 100% digital if you want it that way.
How does the onboarding process work? +
We start with a first meeting — in person or by video — to understand your situation. We then send you a tailored proposal, and once it's approved we get to work. No small print, no abusive lock-ins.
How much do your services cost? +
Pricing varies with complexity and volume of operations. We always send transparent proposals after a detailed review of your business — no hidden fees, no surprise charges.
How do you protect my company's data? +
We take security seriously. We use up-to-date document management platforms, with encryption in transit, access control and regular backups. We comply fully with GDPR and are ready for audits.
How can I optimise my business tax-wise? +
Every case is unique. In a first meeting we review the structure of your business, the current tax regime and where you're heading — and then we walk you through concrete optimisation scenarios: VAT framework, choice between personal and corporate income tax, deduction of costs, sector-specific tax benefits and income planning.

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Data controller

Algarismo Sul, headquartered in Almada (Portugal), is the controller of personal data collected in the context of providing its accounting, tax, payroll and management consulting services.

Contact: geral@algarismosul.pt

2. What data we collect

  • Identification and contact data (name, tax ID, address, email, phone) provided voluntarily.
  • Tax, accounting and employment data required to perform the service agreement.
  • Technical data (IP address, browser type) collected when loading external resources (Google Fonts) — see section 6.

3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Performance of contract — delivery of the services agreed upon (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Compliance with legal obligation — filings with the Portuguese Tax Authority, Social Security and other public bodies (art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Legitimate interest — handling commercial enquiries and managing the client relationship (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Retention periods

Tax and accounting data are kept for 10 years, as required by the Portuguese General Tax Law and other applicable legislation. Contact data not linked to a contractual relationship is deleted after 3 years without interaction.

5. Data subject rights

You may, at any time, exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and objection, by contacting us by email. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority (CNPD — cnpd.pt).

6. Processors and transfers

This site uses Google Fonts, which implies sending the visitor's IP address to Google servers. To deliver our services we may rely on accounting software and cloud storage providers based in the European Union, always under a data-processing agreement pursuant to art. 28 GDPR.

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures — access control, encryption in transit, regular backups — to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration or destruction.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect legal or operational changes. The version in force is always the one published on this page.
